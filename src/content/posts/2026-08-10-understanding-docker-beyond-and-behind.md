Docker - is magic for most of us. It just works. perfect. full-stop.



But, this time, I questioned (or my colleague did?) myself - what's underneath docker? how it works? what is actually the docker images? what it constitutes of layer? and where does it gets images from? I mean, how all these layers come and works so perfectly? like a magic?



Okay, to be honest, I was troubleshooting some \`docker pull\` issue, and so I loop into the thought, what is an image? How it is being pulled? 

I never had this question myself, and if I recall well, I played with first docker container on 2022. It's been almost 4 years, and now, I got an urge to dig through the rabbit hole.



Good news, with some hands-on practice, unwrapping and bundling the images, I found answers to few of my questions, and got more new questions. So, through this series of Understanding the Docker - Beyond and Behind, I will share you all what I found and what I got.



Let's learn together.
